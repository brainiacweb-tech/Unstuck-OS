# Privacy policy — draft

Unstuck OS stores preferences, favorites, recent action identifiers and optional network diagnostic records in your per-user application data folder. Records can include network adapter names, local IP/gateway configuration and timestamps. No telemetry, account, advertising, analytics SDK, cloud database or AI service is used.

Network tests explicitly contact www.microsoft.com through DNS/HTTPS and www.msftconnecttest.com through HTTP. Your network resolver and Microsoft receive normal connection metadata including your IP address. They do not receive your local diagnostic history from this app. Internet connectivity is not required for local search and guidance.

Camera and microphone streams are processed locally for preview and a volume meter. They are never recorded, saved or uploaded by the application. They stop when leaving the test or pressing Stop media. Windows may maintain its own permission/access records.

History can be disabled or cleared in Preferences. Disabling history deletes saved diagnostic records; recent actions can be cleared separately using Clear history. Reset preferences clears favorites and preferences. Uninstall may leave per-user settings; delete the app userData directory for complete removal after closing the app. No passwords, credentials or product keys are collected.

Publisher legal identity, support email and public privacy policy URL: TO BE PROVIDED before publication.


The shortcut Copy action writes the selected known chord to the Windows clipboard and reads it back to verify. Clipboard content is never uploaded or saved. Local error logs inside the preferences JSON contain at most 50 timestamps, operation names and fixed error codes; no raw arguments or diagnostic evidence is logged. History controls clear/disable these logs, and Review locally stored data shows them.
